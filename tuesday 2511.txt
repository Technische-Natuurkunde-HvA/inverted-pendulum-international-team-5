# AS5600 Sensor Issue Report

## Summary

During integration of the AS5600 magnetic encoder, all initial sensors returned constant values of **0.0°**, indicating no valid I2C communication. After systematic testing, the issue was traced not to the sensors themselves, but to soldering damage on the breakout boards.

## Steps Performed

* Tested **three different AS5600 sensors** → all returned 0.0°.
* Replaced all wiring → no improvement.
* Swapped to a different Arduino → still no readings.
* At the beginning of the session, tested a **new AS5600 without soldering** (wires inserted directly) → **sensor worked immediately**.

## Root Cause (Most Likely)

The previous AS5600 boards were **damaged during soldering**. Possible effects:

* overheating of PCB pads or traces,
* accidental short on SDA/SCL,
* broken pad or lifted trace,
* partial or cold solder joints.

## Conclusion

The AS5600 works correctly when not soldered. The repeated failures with soldered modules strongly indicate that soldering operations compromised the sensors.

## Recommendations

* Avoid soldering until tests are fully validated.
* Use pre‑soldered header pins or low‑temperature solder.
* Check continuity (SDA, SCL, VCC, GND) with a multimeter before powering.
* Handle PCB pads carefully to avoid mechanical damage.

*End of concise report.*
